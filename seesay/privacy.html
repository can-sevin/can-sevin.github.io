Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026. The Turkish version is authoritative; this translation is provided for convenience.

In short

  • Your voice recordings never reach us. Your phone’s speech recognition service (Apple or Google) turns your speech into text; only that text is sent to us.
  • Transcripts are evaluated by AI. To prepare scenes, tasks and feedback, transcripts are sent to Anthropic’s Claude. We ask for your explicit consent before this happens.
  • We don’t sell your data or share it with anyone except the service providers needed to run the app.
  • Delete your account in the app: Settings → Hesabımı sil (Delete my account). This permanently removes your account and all learning data.

We do not upload audio. Pronunciation recordings stay on your phone so you can hear yourself. We don’t collect location, contacts, photos or health data, and the app contains no analytics or tracking SDKs other than the ad SDK described below.

3. AI evaluation

Evaluating your speech with AI is the core of SeeSay, so we ask for your explicit consent before your first practice. Without it the app cannot evaluate your speech.

  • Anthropic (Claude) receives your transcripts and the context needed to evaluate them: your level, target language, goal, interests, the skills you are working on and phrases saved in your notebook. Anthropic does not train its models on data sent through its commercial API, and may retain it for a limited period under its own terms to prevent misuse.
  • fal.ai draws scene pictures from a scene description that contains no personal information.

AI can make mistakes. Feedback is educational, and level or IELTS estimates are not official exam results.

4. Service providers

Supabase (database, authentication, storage, server functions), Anthropic (AI), fal.ai (images), RevenueCat (subscriptions), Google AdMob (rewarded ads), and Apple and Google (speech recognition, app stores and payments). Each processes data only as needed for its task and under its own privacy terms. We may also disclose data where required by law.

5. International transfers

These providers operate servers outside Türkiye (mainly in the United States and the EU). Transfers rely on standard contractual safeguards and on what is necessary to provide the service; for AI evaluation we also ask for your explicit consent.

6. Legal bases

Performing our contract with you (account, learning service, subscription); your consent (AI evaluation and transfer abroad, personalised ads and iOS tracking permission); legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, reviewing reports); legal obligations.

7. Ads and tracking

On the free plan you may watch a short ad to unlock an extra scene. In the EEA and UK we ask for consent with Google’s consent form first; on iOS we ask for tracking permission only before your first ad. If you decline, ads are not personalised. Premium members never see these prompts. You can change ad choices in Settings → Reklam gizlilik seçenekleri (shown where required).

8. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. Deleting your account removes your account, profile, transcripts, feedback, notebook, scene pictures and subscription record at RevenueCat; deleted data leaves our backups within the normal backup cycle. Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account. If you can’t open the app, see how to request deletion.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Database rules let each user read only their own records. AI calls and purchase verification run only on our servers, with secret keys.

10. Children

SeeSay is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. Users under 18 should use the app with a parent’s or guardian’s permission.

11. Your rights

Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Art. 11) and the GDPR you may request access to, correction, deletion or portability of your data. You may also object to processing and withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect earlier processing. Write to seesay.support@gmail.com from your account’s email address; we reply within 30 days. You may also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or your local data protection authority.

12. Changes

We will announce material changes in the app. The current version is always published at this address.
