Delete your SeeSay account and data

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open SeeSay and sign in.
  2. Tap the profile icon at the top right to open Ayarlar (Settings).
  3. Scroll to the bottom, tap Hesabımı sil (Delete my account) and confirm.

If you can’t open the app

Email seesay.support@gmail.com from your account’s email address with the subject “Account deletion request”. We verify the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account (email, password, name), learning profile, speech transcripts, scenes and scene pictures, feedback, placement results, notebook, review and pronunciation records, skill progress, hands-free conversations, reports and your subscription record at RevenueCat. Deletion is permanent. Deleted data leaves our backups within the normal backup cycle. Apple and Google may keep payment records as their laws require.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it first in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions to stop being charged.
